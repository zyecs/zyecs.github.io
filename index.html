Zesheng Ye

Greetings! I am a National Intelligence Postdoctoral Fellow at the TMLR Group, Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology, University of Melbourne, hosted by Dr. Feng Liu. I did my Ph.D. studies at the University of New South Wales, feeling fortunate to have been supervised by Prof. Lina Yao.

I study how to make machine learning systems trustworthy across their lifecycle, with a particular emphasis on:

  • Reliable prediction (RP): knowing what models don't know under data scarcity
  • Reliable adaptation (RA): reusing foundation models for new tasks effectively
  • Reliable evaluation (RE): certifying performance and safety claims of deployed models

My long-term goal is to build ML systems whose reliability can be quantified, justified, and verified, such that AI can be safely deployed for the general public with guaranteed confidence.
